Wallpaper Builder

Turns a photo into the raw RGB565 .bin the Glass face's on-device wallpaper picker reads from an SD card — entirely in your browser, no install needed.

1. Choose an image

Output is 320x140 - the clock's digit area only, not the whole screen (the 30px status bar at the top is always solid-colour badges, so that strip of the source image would never show anyway). Same shape as Custom Face's bg.bin.

2. Preview

This is the wallpaper alone - on the clock the Glass digits' real translucency sits on top of it.

3. Download

Copy the downloaded file into a /wallpapers/ folder on the SD card - any filename, subfolders are fine too, the on-device picker just browses whatever's there. You can drop in several and pick between them on the clock.

  1. Insert the SD card, power on the clock.
  2. LEFT/RIGHT-cycle the clock face to Glass.
  3. Hold LEFT to open the wallpaper picker: LEFT/RIGHT moves the selection, OK opens a folder or picks a file, holding OK backs out.

The choice is remembered across reboots. No SD card, or nothing ever picked, and Glass just falls back to its original flat black background - never a blank or broken screen.
