Custom Face Builder

Design a fully custom clock face - your own artwork for every digit (0-9), the colon, an optional background image, and the status-bar colours - then build it into a .cface file. Upload that from the clock's web portal (Custom Faces card) and pick it from Settings > Custom Face on the device. Nothing here touches the clock directly - this page runs entirely offline in your browser.

Every digit image is drawn pixel-exact on the clock, not stretched to fit - whatever size artwork you upload for a digit is the size it shows at (capped to 140px tall; taller images are scaled down to fit). Design your digits at the size you want them to actually appear.

Face name

Shown in the clock's Settings > Custom Face list, and used as the downloaded filename.

Background

Optional. Covers the whole digit area (320×140px) behind your digit artwork. If you don't upload one, the solid colour below fills that area instead - and it's always used as a fallback wherever a background image doesn't reach the top status bar.
